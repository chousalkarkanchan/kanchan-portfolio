How I work · User-centred design

I start with one question.

Who are we designing for, and what do they need to get done?
STAGE 01 · RESEARCH

Understand users & context.

I learn about your business, your audience and the situations they are in, through conversations, user research and competitor reviews.

STAGE 02 · DEFINE

Define needs & goals.

I turn findings into clear user needs, personas and goals, so every design decision has a reason behind it.

STAGE 03 · DESIGN

Ideate & design.

I map user flows and page structure, then shape wireframes into responsive, on-brand interfaces.

STAGE 04 · EVALUATE

Prototype & test.

I build interactive prototypes, test key tasks with real users, and refine whatever slows them down.

STAGE 05 · DELIVER

Deliver & iterate.

I hand off developer-ready designs or build the site, then keep improving it based on feedback and results.
